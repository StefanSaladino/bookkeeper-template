Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal information Williams & Williams Accounting Solutions Inc. collects, why it is needed, how it is protected and the choices available to you.

Effective August 16, 2026Last updated August 17, 2026

Williams & Williams Accounting Solutions Inc. (“Williams & Williams,” “we,” “us” or “our”) is responsible for personal information under its control. We handle personal information in accordance with applicable Canadian privacy law, including the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (“PIPEDA”) where it applies.

1. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to personal information collected through our website, email, telephone calls, consultations, client onboarding and bookkeeping services. It does not apply to anonymized information that cannot reasonably identify an individual, or to third-party websites and services that operate under their own privacy policies.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Contact information, such as your name, email address, telephone number, company name and role.
  • Inquiry information, including the service you are interested in and details you choose to share about your business.
  • Client and bookkeeping information, such as billing details, transaction records, financial statements, tax-account information, payroll records, supporting documents and authorized user details when you engage us.
  • Communications, including emails, meeting notes, support requests, approvals and records of your preferences.

Please do not submit banking credentials, social insurance numbers or other highly sensitive information through the public website form or ordinary email. Clients receive instructions for approved secure document-sharing methods.

Information collected automatically

Our hosting and security providers may automatically process limited technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, requested pages, timestamps, referring page and diagnostic or security events. We use this information to deliver the website, maintain security, prevent misuse and understand basic site performance.

3. How we use personal information

We may use personal information to:

  • respond to inquiries and schedule consultations;
  • assess whether our services are suitable for your business;
  • provide, administer and improve bookkeeping and related support;
  • authenticate authorized users and maintain secure client records;
  • prepare invoices, process payments and maintain business records;
  • communicate about deadlines, missing information and service changes;
  • meet legal, tax, regulatory, insurance and professional obligations;
  • protect our systems, clients and business from error, fraud or security threats; and
  • send marketing communications when we have consent or are otherwise permitted by law.

We do not use personal information for purposes that are materially different from those identified at collection without obtaining additional consent, unless permitted or required by law.

4. Consent and your choices

By providing personal information, you consent to its collection, use and disclosure for the purposes described at the time of collection and in this policy. The form of consent may vary depending on the sensitivity of the information and the circumstances.

You may withdraw consent to an optional use at any time, subject to legal or contractual restrictions and reasonable notice. Withdrawing consent may prevent us from providing a requested service. Service messages that are necessary to administer an active engagement are not marketing messages.

Where we send commercial electronic messages, we include our identification and a working unsubscribe method. We process unsubscribe requests promptly and retain only the information reasonably necessary to honour the request.

5. When we share information

We do not sell or rent personal information. We may disclose it only as reasonably necessary to:

  • Service providers that support secure hosting, email, document management, accounting software, payroll, payments, scheduling, data backup and professional operations.
  • Your authorized professionals, such as your accountant, tax preparer, lawyer, payroll provider or other representative, when you instruct us or the engagement requires coordination.
  • Authorities or other parties when required by law, court order or lawful process, or when reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, enforce an agreement, protect rights or respond to a security incident.
  • A successor organization in connection with a proposed or completed sale, financing or reorganization, subject to appropriate confidentiality and lawful handling requirements.

Service providers may process information in Canada, the United States or another jurisdiction. Information processed outside your province or Canada may be accessible to courts, law-enforcement bodies or regulators under the laws of that jurisdiction. We require providers to use information only for authorized purposes and to provide appropriate protection.

6. Cookies and analytics

This website currently uses only technology necessary to deliver pages, maintain security and support basic functions. It does not currently use advertising cookies or third-party behavioural analytics. If we add non-essential analytics, advertising technology or a newsletter tracking tool, we will update this policy and provide any notice or consent mechanism required by applicable law.

7. Retention

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the identified purpose, an active or potential engagement, and applicable legal, tax, insurance, professional or contractual requirements.

  • General inquiries that do not become clients are normally retained for up to 24 months after the last meaningful contact.
  • Client and financial records are retained for the engagement and the period required by applicable law or our documented retention schedule.
  • Security and server logs are normally retained for a limited period appropriate to troubleshooting, fraud prevention and security monitoring.
  • Unsubscribe records may be retained as necessary to ensure that we continue to honour your preference.

When information is no longer required, we securely delete, destroy or anonymize it, subject to technical backup cycles and lawful preservation requirements.

8. Safeguards and financial information

We use administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information. These may include role-based access, multi-factor authentication, encryption in transit, secure document portals, vendor due diligence, confidentiality obligations, backups and procedures for detecting and responding to incidents.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we will investigate and provide notice to affected individuals and regulators as required by applicable law.

9. Access and correction

You may request access to personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information. We may need to verify your identity before responding. In limited circumstances, applicable law may permit or require us to refuse access; if so, we will explain the reason unless legally prohibited.

To make a request, contact the Privacy Officer using the details below. We will respond within the period required by applicable law and may charge only a fee permitted by law after providing advance notice.

10. Children’s privacy

Our website and services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14 through this website. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

11. External links

Our website may link to third-party websites. We do not control their privacy or security practices and encourage you to review their policies before providing personal information.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices, service providers or legal obligations. The revised policy will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date. We will provide additional notice when a change materially affects how we handle information already collected.
